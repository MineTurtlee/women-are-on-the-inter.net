Women are on the internet

the problem

people keep assuming the other party's gender as male, unknowingly online.
while this is normal for most people, people who aren't men may feel excluded even if it was unintentional.

the solution

instead of using (somewhat) exclusive language, you can help by trying not to use:

  • bro (or brother)
  • (hey) guys
and instead, use:
  • everyone
  • all
  • (when asking a question, omit the exclusive words)

    this helps!

by doing this, you're doing well by using inclusive language to make everyone, regardless genders feel included!
